FAQ

I bought something but it didn't unlock.

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases (iOS). On Android, make sure you're signed in to the Google account you bought with and restart the game. See In-App Purchases.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Premium in the store. It removes the ads between levels; the optional rewarded ads (extra life, 2× coins, free coins) stay available if you want them.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the App, resetting progress in Settings, or moving to a new device starts a new save. Premium and power balls can be restored; coins cannot.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google — see Refunds.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the App, or use Reset progress in Settings. We don't keep any of your data on our side; see the Privacy Policy.
