Privacy Policy

Rebound by GT Dev

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GT Dev ("we", "us") handles information when you play Rebound (the "App") on iOS or Android. We do not run accounts or servers for the App, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cleared levels, stars, best times, coins, balls and power-ups you own, your settings, and whether you bought Premium) is saved only on your device. We do not collect it or send it to any server. Deleting the App, or using Reset progress in Settings, removes it.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (with the ironSource Ads and Unity Ads networks). To show and measure ads, these partners may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system, language, carrier and connection type;
  • ad interactions (views, clicks, rewarded ads completed) and diagnostic data.

They use this to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, show ads based on your interests. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity Ads, LevelPlay and ironSource)
  • ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy

On iOS you can choose whether apps may track you (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. Buying Premium removes the ads shown between levels; rewarded ads you choose to watch remain available.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The App receives only a confirmation of what was bought (product and transaction ID) so it can unlock it on your device. See In-App Purchases.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data, and the App has no sign-in, chat or social features. If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to answer you and delete them when no longer needed.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, most requests are handled by the ad partners above or by limiting ad tracking on your device; we are happy to help — contact us at the address below.

7. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Data collected by our ad partners is kept according to their policies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
