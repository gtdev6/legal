In-App Purchases

Rebound by GT Dev

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Rebound is free to play. Everything below is optional. Prices are set by the App Store or Google Play in your local currency and are always shown in the game before you buy. Rebound has no subscriptions and nothing renews automatically.

What you can buy

Coins are spent in the in-game store on balls and power-ups. They can also be earned for free by playing and by watching optional rewarded ads. Virtual items have no cash value.

Restore purchases

  • iPhone / iPad: open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.
  • Android: purchases come back automatically when you are signed in to the same Google account.

Premium and power balls can be restored. Coin packs are used up when bought and cannot be restored.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: request a refund on Google Play

Parental controls

You can block or require approval for purchases with Screen Time on iOS (Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases) or with purchase authentication in the Google Play settings.

Problems with a purchase?

If something you paid for didn't unlock, try Restore purchases first, then contact support with your device and the store receipt.
